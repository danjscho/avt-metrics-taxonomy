# v5.0.3 — cadence audit triage

User-noticed inconsistency: TP.ASR-13 Numeric Accuracy was labelled `Measurement Cadence: One-off gate` but its body explicitly described monthly review of production-traffic numeric accuracy + a sustained-drift alert + a pause-on-confirmed-dosage-error trigger in production traffic. Cadence label contradicted body content.

A scan found 51 metrics labelled `One-off gate` in the catalogue; 21 of them also listed `Periodic Audit` or `Continuous` in their `Lifecycle Phases` dimension — looking like a widespread under-claiming.

This file is the per-metric verdict on all 51 (50 after the v5.0.3 phase (a) fix to TP.ASR-13). For each metric: read body — particularly Trigger Conditions, Operational Specification, Lifecycle Phases — and decide:

- **Keep** — body genuinely supports one-off-gate-only. The metric is measured pre-deployment and not re-measured continuously / periodically in any meaningful operational sense. The cadence label is correct.
- **Change** — body has explicit periodic-audit or continuous-monitoring triggers (re-run on component change, monthly review, drift alerts, etc.). Cadence label is wrong; should be `Periodic audit` or `Continuous`.
- **Multi-cadence (defer)** — body describes a hard pre-deployment gate AND ongoing periodic measurement, but in a way the single-value Cadence dimension cannot express. Lifecycle Phases lists both Pre-deployment + Periodic Audit/Continuous but body is genuinely pre-deployment-focused; the Lifecycle Phases value is more aspirational than substantive. Flag for v5.1+ structural fix where the Cadence dimension becomes two fields (or where Lifecycle Phases is corrected to match body intent).

## Headline result

After examining all 50 remaining one-off-gate metrics:

- **Change: 1** — TP.ASR-12 Hallucination-Under-Noise Rate (applied in v5.0.3 alongside TP.ASR-13)
- **Multi-cadence (defer): 19** — Lifecycle Phases includes Periodic/Continuous but the body content is pre-deployment-focused; Lifecycle Phases is the outlier, not Cadence. The honest fix is either tightening Lifecycle Phases on each metric, or splitting the Cadence dimension structurally — both v5.1+ work.
- **Keep: 30** — body and label and Lifecycle Phases all agree on one-off-gate semantics.

So the *immediate* metric-content change in v5.0.3 is **2 metrics** (TP.ASR-13 in phase a; TP.ASR-12 here). The triage surfaces a wider structural concern — `Measurement Cadence` is a single value, but many metrics are genuinely both pre-deployment-gated AND periodically-audited — and recommends addressing it as a v5.1+ workstream.

## Cross-cluster observations

- **TP.WB-1 and TP.WB-3** are the cleanest examples of the structural problem. They have a hard pre-deployment gate (safety-critical fidelity = 100%) AND continuous monitoring (≥99% monthly safety-critical fidelity). The single-value Cadence dimension forces the metric to pick one; Lifecycle Phases legitimately lists both. This is the v5.1+ structural-fix candidate.
- **GV.PD-10** has the same shape — pre-deployment capability test AND ongoing per-SAR tracking.
- **The 19 "Multi-cadence (defer)" verdicts** mostly involve Lifecycle Phases that include Periodic Audit / Continuous *aspirationally* — i.e. the metric *should* be re-audited but the body doesn't operationalise that. Either the body needs filling out or the Lifecycle Phases needs trimming. Each is a small content edit; together they're a v5.1+ batch.

## Recommendation for v5.1+

Two-step approach when the work resumes:

**Step 1: tighten 17 Lifecycle Phases values** — for the multi-cadence-deferred metrics where the Lifecycle Phases includes Periodic Audit / Continuous but the body is pre-deployment-only, trim Lifecycle Phases to `Pre-deployment` only. This eliminates the visible inconsistency without changing operational behaviour.

**Step 2: structurally split Cadence on the 2 genuine multi-cadence metrics** (TP.WB-1, TP.WB-3, GV.PD-10) — for these the body genuinely describes both gates and ongoing monitoring; the single-value Cadence dimension is the wrong shape. Options:
- Allow `Measurement Cadence` to be multi-valued (e.g. "Pre-deployment gate; Continuous monitoring")
- Split into two columns: `Pre-deployment Gate (yes/no)` + `Ongoing Cadence (Continuous / Periodic / None)`

The first option is smaller (no breaking change to CSV/JSON structure; just allows commas in the value). The second is structurally cleaner. Defer the choice until v5.1+ work starts.

## Honest caveats

- **Author re-reading author.** Same caveat as the v4.6 threshold review and other Phase 1 audits — I'm one author state re-reading another's labels. Some "Keep" verdicts on Tier 3 research metrics are defensible because the body genuinely says "research only, no deployment monitoring"; a deployer-feedback round might shift more verdicts.
- **TP.AC-3 was originally flagged Change but reclassified Multi-cadence-defer** because the body simply doesn't have the Trigger Conditions / Operational Specification content that would justify a periodic-audit label. Adding it would be a body content edit, not a label fix.
- **Lifecycle Phases is doing inconsistent work across the catalogue.** Some metrics use it to describe what *should* happen; others to describe what *is* operationalised in body. That's the deeper inconsistency this audit surfaces.
