## How to look these up

For C2 and C3, the academic search path is:

- **ACL Anthology** (https://aclanthology.org/) — searchable archive of ACL/EMNLP/SIGDIAL/etc. papers. Free to read; DOIs are stable.
- **arxiv.org** — most NLP papers also live here as preprints. arxiv URLs (https://arxiv.org/abs/YYMM.NNNNN) are very stable and snapshot-able.
- **DBLP** (https://dblp.org/) — comprehensive CS bibliography with author + venue search.
- **Google Scholar** — title search, then click through to ACL/arxiv where possible (avoid academic.oup.com or paywalled-only links unless that's the only canonical version).

Preference order for the catalogue URL: **DOI** (publisher) → **arxiv.org abstract page** → **ACL Anthology page** → **publisher landing page** → **GitHub project page**. Avoid PubMed for purely-CS work; use it for clinical-content papers.
