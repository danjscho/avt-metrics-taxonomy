## Honest scope ceiling

The FTS notice is the public-tender description, not the full Atamis application pack. The application pack itself is where the granular per-criterion attestation form + the Performance and Monitoring Response document template would sit. Without Atamis credentials we can't see those. **This action list is defensible against the public FTS surface; it is NOT defensible against the granular Atamis pack we don't have.** The Atamis pack ceiling stands as a known limitation.

What the FTS notice DOES authoritatively name:
- **DTAC** + everything DTAC transitively names (DSPT, Cyber Essentials, FHIR R4, WCAG 2.2 AA, AIS, NHS Number/PDS, ICO registration, DCB0129)
- **MHRA Class 1 medical device + post-market surveillance**
- **"the guidance issued by NHS England"** (transitively: NHSE IG Guidance March 2026, CIO/CCIO guidance, adopting-ambient-scribing pages)

What the FTS notice does NOT name (audited in Phase 1b but Registry-deferrable):
- NHS LLM Framework, NICE ESF (ECD7), NHS T.E.S.T., PSIRF, PRSB

This split — Registry-required vs Registry-deferrable — is the foundation of the priority ordering below.

## Three honest concerns about this list

### 1. The 10 promotions are author-judgement against transitive framework analysis

A deployer-feedback round would shift some. The clearest case is the `GV.SC-1/-2/-6` cybersecurity cluster: MHRA WP5 says cybersecurity is gate-level, but AVT-specific adversarial-prompt-injection testing isn't industry-standard yet. The FTS notice is silent on cybersecurity testing cadence specifically. Defensible to promote to Tier 1 on MHRA WP5 logic; equally defensible to keep at Tier 2 and only promote when industry adversarial-testing matures. **Worth confirming before Phase 5 lands these specifically.**

### 2. The 2 new mints are governance-attestation-shape

`GV.PD-17 Information Asset Register Completeness` and `GV.VT-16 Joint-Controller Status Assessment` are both more "process attestations" than "measurements" (similar shape to `GV.CR-7 DPIA Template Completion Rate`). If the v5.x rule is "metrics must be measurable, not just attestations", these arguably belong as a separate catalogue entry kind. The taxonomy already has `GV.CR-*` precedent for compliance-attestation-as-metric; defensible to mint as metrics but worth the explicit framing decision.

### 3. The Atamis application pack ceiling still stands

If Atamis access becomes possible later and the granular pack reveals different priorities (e.g. specific bias-testing cadence in the Performance & Monitoring document template; specific incident-reporting timelines), the Phase 5 verdicts here might shift. The mitigation: ship Phase 5 against the public surface, document the Atamis ceiling honestly, and re-run Phase 5 verdicts when the pack becomes accessible.

- Update `_standards-mapping.md` per-framework sections to reflect the new metrics replacing the gap candidates
- Per-metric `Change history:` stanzas on all promotions (10 stanzas)

**Prep work** (could ship as v5.2.1 or as part of v5.3.0):
- Update `_gaps.md` to mark the 18 pulled-through items as "promoted in v5.3" (preserve the rows; don't delete) — same shape as the v4.1 deprecation/decommissioning promotions
- Update `_standards-mapping.md` per-framework tables to point to new metric refs

**Big-bang concern.** v5.3.0 lands ~28 metric content changes simultaneously. This is the largest content-change batch since the early v2.x consolidation. Two ways to mitigate:

- **(i) Stage across multiple MINOR releases** — promotions in v5.3, pull-throughs in v5.4, mints in v5.5. Slower but each release reviewable independently. Better if anything goes wrong.
- **(ii) Big-bang v5.3 with rigorous per-metric verification** — apply v4.2/v4.3 Pass A + Pass B style verification on each new metric body before merge. Slower per-metric but ships in one release.

My recommendation is **(ii)** — the metrics being promoted are already verified (existing catalogue entries), the pull-throughs have well-defined `_gaps.md` rationale, and the mints are small. A single careful release with per-metric verification is cleaner than 3 staged releases.
