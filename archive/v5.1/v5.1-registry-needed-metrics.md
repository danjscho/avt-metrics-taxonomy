# What metrics the AVT Registry transitively needs

A small summary pulled out of the v5.0.1 Phase 1a audit. **Rough list of the metrics the taxonomy thinks any Registry-certified AVT supplier / deploying NHS organisation will need to operationalise**, based on the publicly-readable surface (CIO/CCIO guidance + IG guidance March 2026 + Mills & Reeve legal summary). Will sharpen when the actual Registry attestation form is accessible.

## Honest framing on this list

- **Mapped against the transitive surface, not the Registry form itself.** The Registry's actual attestation criteria are not publicly enumerated; they live behind the National Commercial and Procurement Hub. This list is the union of what the Registry-references-frameworks ask for, interpreted as what the Registry self-certification therefore covers.
- **"Needed" means present in the catalogue and Registry-relevant** — not "every supplier must operationalise all of these on day one". The Calibration & Context principle still applies: deployment-context-dependent operationalisation, with local calibration before contractual use.
- **All Tier 1 metrics carry the "starting points, not standards" framing per v5.0.** Specific numerical thresholds live on the [Threshold Reference](https://danjscho.github.io/avt-metrics-taxonomy/thresholds/) page with structural caveats; metric bodies carry only qualitative trigger conditions.
- **Compound-errors caveat applies.** A deployer adopting all of these as written without local calibration may sit on overlapping triggers. Treat as *one input* to a clinical-safety judgement, not a pass/fail gate.

## Summary count

**38 Tier 1 metrics** are needed to cover the transitive Registry surface. The current Tier 1 set has 45 metrics, so:

- **38 of the 45 current Tier 1 metrics** are confirmed Registry-relevant
- **7 current Tier 1 metrics** are not directly named by the transitive surface — they sit on the taxonomy's own clinical-safety logic (e.g. `TP.ASR-13` numeric accuracy is implied by SaMD safety but not explicitly required by Registry-named frameworks; `HL.HF-1` trajectory-based edit rate is taxonomy-proposed). These stay Tier 1 per the locked rule (no demotions; the minimum set extends Tier 1, doesn't curate it).
- **0 non-Tier-1 metrics surface as Registry-required** in the current Tier 1 set — but **4 candidates from Tier 2/3** were flagged for promotion discussion (GV.VT-2, GV.VT-14, IO.FE-1, TP.ASR-4); confirming or rejecting those needs the actual Registry form.

## What this list does *not* cover (yet)

These are gaps surfaced in v5.0.1 but not in the catalogue today. They will need decision-points once the Registry form is available:

1. **Lawful-basis documentation attestation** (UK GDPR Art 6/9 alignment) — partially covered by GV.PD cluster but no single attestation
2. **Right to Restrict Processing handling** — distinct from Right to Erasure
3. **Information asset register / storage-location documentation** — required by IG guidance
4. **Joint-controller status assessment** — when supplier determines processing aspects
5. **Organisational opt-out mechanism** — for non-direct-care use cases
6. **Training data provenance disclosure** — implied by CIO/CCIO guidance
7. **Encryption-specific attestation** — currently implicit in GV.SC security cluster
8. **AVT-specific organisational policy attestation**

Plus four already-roadmapped gaps:
- `GV.SG-19` Systems-Based Incident Analysis (PSIRF)
- `GV.CR-12` Board-Level AI Governance
- `GV.CR-14` Consultation-Type Appropriateness (Caldicott)
- `TP.WB-11` PRSB Semantic Completeness

## When the Registry form is available

Re-run Phase 1a as a diff against this list. Three classes of finding:

- **Confirmed**: Registry directly names what's already covered → keep as Tier 1 / promote where flagged.
- **Surplus**: this list includes things the Registry form doesn't actually require → re-evaluate Tier 1 status.
- **Net gap**: Registry asks for something not on this list → mint or promote from `_gaps.md`.

Until then, this list is the working answer to "what does the Registry transitively need from the taxonomy?"
