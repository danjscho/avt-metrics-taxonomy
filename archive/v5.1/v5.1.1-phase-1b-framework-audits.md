## NEW gap candidates (verified absent from `_gaps.md`)

Cross-checked against `_gaps.md`'s 9 sections (§1a/b/c, §2a-h, §3a, §4a/b/c, §5+). Two candidates survived:

1. **Information asset register / storage-location documentation** — surfaced in NHSE IG Guidance audit. The IG guidance explicitly requires an information asset register documenting AVT data storage at each processing stage, retention timelines, and provider responsibilities. Currently no taxonomy metric covers this. Process-attestation flavour, not a measurement; could mint as a Tier 1 compliance attestation metric (e.g. `GV.PD-XX Information Asset Register Completeness`). **Verified absent from `_gaps.md`.** Source: NHSE IG Guidance section 8.

2. **Joint-controller status assessment** — surfaced in NHSE IG Guidance audit. UK GDPR Article 26 makes joint-controller status applicable when supplier determines significant processing means/purposes. Currently subsumed under DPSA / sub-processor framework (`GV.VT-7 Sub-Processor Transparency`) but no explicit joint-controller-test metric. Verifying joint-controller status is a binding-or-not-binding determination distinct from sub-processor disclosure. Mint candidate: `GV.VT-XX Joint-Controller Status Assessment` (Tier 2). **Verified absent from `_gaps.md`.** Source: NHSE IG Guidance section 5; UK GDPR Art 26.

## Observations + recommendations

### 1. The taxonomy's transitive Registry coverage is structurally complete

After Phase 1a (Registry transitive map: 14 frameworks all in catalogue) + Phase 1b batch 1 (4 frameworks deeply audited: 128 sub-criteria, only 2 NEW gaps), the answer to *"does the AVT taxonomy cover what the Registry's transitively-named frameworks require?"* is **yes, structurally** — modulo:

- **15 already-roadmapped gaps** waiting for Phase 5 promotion / mint
- **2 NEW gaps** flagged in this batch (information asset register; joint-controller assessment)
- **8 Tier 2/3 → Tier 1 promotion candidates** awaiting user decision
- **Phase 1b batches 2 + 3** still to run on the remaining 8 frameworks (NHS LLM, DSPT, NICE ESF, T.E.S.T., GDPR, CQC, FHIR, ICO) — likely to surface a small number more

Phase 1b batches 2 + 3 are unlikely to dramatically change the headline finding. The taxonomy was substantively built against these frameworks; the audit is largely confirming that.

### 2. The Phase 5 list is now stable

For when the user is ready to make Phase 5 decisions:

**Pull-throughs (existing `_gaps.md` items)**: 15 candidates listed above.

**New mints**: 2 (information asset register; joint-controller assessment) — both Tier 1/2 governance attestations.

**Promotions Tier 2/3 → Tier 1**: 8 candidates listed above.

**Total Phase 5 surface**: ~25 metric-content changes (15 promotions/pull-throughs + 2 mints + 8 demoted-tier-promotions). Counts after Phase 5 would land roughly **221 → 235-240 metrics**, **45 → 55-60 Tier 1**.

### 3. Honest limitations

- **The actual Registry attestation form is still not publicly accessible.** Phase 1b is auditing the *transitively-named frameworks*, not the Registry's specific attestation criteria. Some Phase 1b coverage may turn out to be over-strict (Registry doesn't require everything its transitively-named frameworks ask for), and some surplus may surface as "useful but not Registry-required". The Phase 5 prioritisation step needs the actual Registry form.

- **Each per-framework audit was author-judgement on the framework-mapping** plus body-content reading + `_gaps.md` cross-check. A deployer-feedback round (or Phase 1b batches 2 + 3) would shift some verdicts.

- **No metric content edits in v5.1.1.** This file is research output that feeds Phase 5 decisions when the user is ready.
