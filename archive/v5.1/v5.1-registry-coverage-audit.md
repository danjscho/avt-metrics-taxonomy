# v5.1 Phase 1a — NHSE AVT Registry coverage audit

**Status:** scoping deliverable. No metric content edits in v5.1; this file feeds Phase 1b (transitive expansion across DTAC / DSPT / DCB / etc.) and Phase 5 (gap promotions).

## Source surface

Three primary documents — what I could fetch publicly:

1. **[NHSE-AVT-Registry] page** at `digital.nhs.uk/services/ambient-scribing/ambient-voice-technology-self-certified-supplier-registry` — the public registry listing. **The page does not expose the attestation criteria themselves**; it only lists the 23 currently listed suppliers and points readers at the National Commercial and Procurement Hub for evidence (which is not publicly viewable at that URL).
2. **CIO/CCIO guidance** at `england.nhs.uk/publication/guidance-on-the-use-of-ai-enabled-ambient-scribing-products/` — names the safety / regulatory considerations CIOs/CCIOs must verify; is the substantive surface from which the registry attestations transitively follow.
3. **IG guidance** at `transform.england.nhs.uk/information-governance/guidance/using-ai-enabled-ambient-scribing-products-in-health-and-care-settings/` — the substantive IG/GDPR/DPIA surface for AVT.

**Honest note on scope.** The Registry page itself doesn't publicly enumerate the attestation criteria. What follows below is a coverage audit against the *transitive surface* — what the CIO/CCIO + IG guidance say organisations and suppliers must address — interpreted as what the Registry self-certification therefore covers. The actual Registry attestation form (held in the National Commercial and Procurement Hub) is not publicly accessible without supplier credentials, so a follow-up pass would refine this against the full form if/when accessible.

## Honest limitations of this Phase 1a

1. **The actual Registry attestation form was not publicly accessible.** What I mapped is the *transitive surface* — what the published CIO/CCIO + IG guidance *says* must be addressed, interpreted as what the Registry self-certification therefore covers. If a supplier or NHS organisation can share the actual attestation form, this audit can be sharpened.
2. **Some "requirements" are process attestations, not metrics.** "Has a clinical safety case" is a process attestation; *measuring* the safety case's completeness is what the metric does (`GV.CR-6`). I've tried to flag where this distinction matters but a follow-up review by someone close to NHS procurement would refine the line.
3. **The framework version basis is a snapshot.** DTAC v2 / DSPT (annual) / LLM Framework v0.2.2 are the current versions referenced; framework updates will require this audit to be re-run periodically.
4. **The promotion-to-Tier-1 candidates are author judgement.** Flagged for discussion, not auto-applied — per the locked v5.x ground rule (extension, not replacement; discuss promotions explicitly).
