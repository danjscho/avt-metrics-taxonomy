## How to annotate

For each entry, replace the `[ ]` action box with one of:

- `[✓]` — URL is correct as-is, accept
- `[!] <new-url>` — URL is wrong, here's the correct one (I'll patch with this URL)
- `[?]` — uncertain, please re-research and propose alternatives
- `[skip]` — drop this entry from the catalogue (no real source exists or it shouldn't be a separate handle)
- `[handle: <new-handle>]` — keep the entry but rename the handle

Free-text after the action is welcome — I'll read it. If you want to leave a note for the discipline-representative choice (e.g. "use Yu et al. 2024 instead of Guo et al. 2017"), just write it inline.

When done, ping me and I'll process the file. Anything left as `[ ]` I'll treat as "still needs review" and surface back.
