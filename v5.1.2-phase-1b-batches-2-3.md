## Honest scope notes

- **The actual Registry attestation form is still not publicly accessible.** All Phase 1b audits worked the *transitively-named frameworks*, not the Registry's specific attestation criteria. Some Phase 1b coverage may turn out to be over-strict (Registry doesn't require everything its transitively-named frameworks ask for), and some surplus may surface as "useful but not Registry-required". The Phase 5 prioritisation step needs the actual Registry form to land.

- **Each per-framework audit was author-judgement** combining framework-mapping + body content reading + `_gaps.md` cross-check. A deployer-feedback round would shift some verdicts. Given the cross-framework synergies above, the gap candidates that surface across multiple frameworks (e.g. `GV.CR-12`, `GV.SG-19`, `TP.WB-11`) are the highest-confidence Phase 5 priorities.

- **`_gaps.md` is the single source of truth for "already roadmapped"**. Every "new gap" candidate flagged in Phase 1b was cross-checked against `_gaps.md` before classification. Only 2 candidates survived (information asset register + joint-controller assessment, both from batch 1 NHSE IG audit).

- **No metric content edits in v5.1.2.** This file is research output that feeds Phase 5 decisions when the user is ready.
